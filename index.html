Breakthrough — a DMT experience simulation

BREAKTHROUGH

A visual and sonic interpretation of the DMT experience, built from what people commonly report: the buzzing, the room breathing, the world melting, and the arrival.

Wear headphones. Use a big screen in a dark room.

Hold left click (or Space) to take a hit (on a phone, press and hold the circle). Move the mouse to look around the room. After the breakthrough, hold and drag (mouse or finger) to turn all the way round. W and S change speed. R for a new world, Esc to come back, M to mute.

An artistic simulation, not a recommendation to use any substance. The Wiki records what studies and people report, including reported doses and hits. Real experiences vary enormously, and this one contains flashing light, strobing, and loud rising sound.
